Weight management does not need to be complicated. Focus on realistic changes you can maintain over time.

⚖️

Aim for Gradual Progress

Sustainable weight management is usually about consistent habits rather than extreme diets or trying to change everything at once.

A useful general target

NHS guidance suggests people trying to lose weight can aim for around 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) per week.

Your ideal rate can depend on your starting weight, health, medications and individual circumstances.

🚶

Walking Is a Great Place to Start

Walking is a simple way to increase your physical activity. You can start gradually and build your activity as your fitness improves.

👟

Walk More

Add short walks throughout your day instead of relying on one long session.

📈

Build Gradually

Increase your activity gradually rather than suddenly making a very large jump.

🔥

Stay Consistent

A routine you can maintain is more useful than an occasional extreme workout.

General UK guidance: adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity each week, or 75 minutes of vigorous activity, alongside muscle-strengthening activity on at least 2 days a week.
🥗

Food Quality Still Matters

Weight loss requires using more energy than you consume over time, but healthy eating is about more than simply reducing calories.

🥦 Fruit & Vegetables

Include a variety of fruit and vegetables in your meals.

🍗 Protein

Include protein foods such as fish, eggs, beans, lentils, lean meat or other suitable alternatives.

🌾 Higher-Fibre Foods

Choose higher-fibre and wholegrain options where practical.

🍬 Watch High-Calorie Extras

Foods and drinks high in sugar and fat can add significant calories without always keeping you full.

💧

Remember Your Fluids

Staying hydrated is part of a healthy lifestyle. The Eatwell Guide uses 6 to 8 cups or glasses of fluid a day as a general guide, although individual needs vary.

Water is an easy choice. Lower-fat milk and lower-sugar or sugar-free drinks can also contribute to fluid intake.

🌾

Don't Forget Fibre

UK guidance recommends adults aim for around 30g of fibre per day as part of a balanced diet.

Useful sources include wholegrains, oats, beans, lentils, chickpeas, vegetables and fruit.

📏

Weight Isn't the Only Measurement

BMI can be useful as a general screening measure, but it does not distinguish between muscle and body fat.

Waist-to-height ratio can provide additional information about fat carried around the abdomen. NHS guidance suggests trying to keep your waist measurement below half your height.

Your Simple Daily Checklist

✅ Work towards your walking target
✅ Include vegetables or fruit
✅ Include a source of protein
✅ Drink regularly throughout the day
✅ Reduce long periods of sitting
✅ Focus on consistency, not perfection
