How do we estimate walking calories?

We estimate walking distance from step count and step length, then walking duration from your chosen speed. Calorie estimates use approximate MET (metabolic equivalent) values for level-ground walking. One MET represents resting energy expenditure.

Gross calories include resting energy during the walk. Net activity calories subtract that resting component. Neither number is a precise prediction of weight change.

How many calories does walking 5,000 or 10,000 steps burn?

There is no single answer. A person's weight, pace and step length change the estimate. Enter 5,000 or 10,000 steps above to see an illustrative result for your own details.

Tips for a realistic walking routine

  • Start from your current comfortable level and increase gradually.
  • Choose a pace at which you can still talk, if suitable for your health and fitness.
  • Remember that food intake, sleep and consistency also affect weight management.
  • Track your progress over time rather than treating a calorie estimate as an exact measurement.

Frequently asked questions

Is this walking calories calculator free?

Yes. It works in your browser and does not require an account.

Is walking 10,000 steps enough to lose weight?

There is no universal step target that guarantees weight loss. Your overall activity and energy intake both matter. Read our daily steps guide.

Are the results exact?

No. The tool estimates level-ground walking using typical speeds and energy costs. Hills, carrying loads and individual differences can change actual expenditure.
